# NotchFlow v0.1.0

NotchFlow v0.1.0 is the first public developer preview of the native macOS notch utility.

## Highlights

- Floating notch panel with hover expansion, click pinning, auto hide, and global hotkey support.
- Now Playing controls, weather and battery glance, screen health reminders, clipboard history, quick launch, wallpaper refresh, and AI token usage summaries.
- Experimental charge limit support with a bundled SMC helper.
- New README, changelog, GitHub Pages site, logo, and app icon assets for the first public release.

## Install

Download `NotchFlow-v0.1.0-macOS.zip`, unzip it, and move `NotchFlow.app` to `/Applications`.

This preview is locally signed but not notarized. On first launch, macOS may require right-clicking the app and choosing **Open**.

## Known Limitations

- Weather, automation, and startup behavior depend on macOS permissions.
- Charge limit controls are experimental and may vary by Mac hardware.
- The app is distributed as a simple zip for this preview; notarized packaging is planned for a later release.
